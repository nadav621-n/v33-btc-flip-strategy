## Key Context: The SOL Number

SOL buy-and-hold dropped 97% during the 2022 FTX collapse. The strategy's max drawdown was −29% — because when SOL started trending down, the bot flipped short and traded the downtrend rather than holding through the collapse.

The gap between strategy (+3,779%) and buy-and-hold (+80%) is wider on SOL than any other asset precisely because of this event.
